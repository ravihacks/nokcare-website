
# NOK Care — Design Guidelines

## Brand Identity

The website must feel:
- Professional
- Warm and approachable
- Modern and premium
- Trustworthy
- Inclusive and accessible

## Colour Palette

Primary Gold: #CD9F03
Warm White: #F8F5EF
Charcoal: #292B30
Pure White: #FFFFFF

Use gold for accents and calls to action.
Use warm white for main backgrounds.
Use charcoal for headings and readable text.
Use pure white for content cards.

Do not use gold for long paragraphs.

## Typography

Primary font: Montserrat
Fallback: Arial, sans-serif

Headings:
- Bold or semibold
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Clear, concise wording

Body:
- Regular weight
- Comfortable line height
- 16–18px standard desktop body text

Use fluid responsive heading sizes.

## Layout

- Maximum content width: 1200px
- Mobile-first responsive layout
- Generous whitespace
- Consistent section spacing
- Asymmetric layouts where appropriate
- Avoid overly crowded sections

## Buttons

Primary:
- Gold background
- Charcoal text
- Medium rounded corners
- Clear hover and focus states

Secondary:
- Transparent or white background
- Charcoal text
- Gold or charcoal border

## Cards

- White backgrounds
- Subtle border or shadow
- Rounded corners
- Consistent internal padding
- Readable titles and descriptions

## Images

Use authentic, inclusive photography related
to disability and aged care services.

Avoid generic corporate imagery.
Do not use images without appropriate rights.
Optimise all image assets for web delivery.

## Homepage Sections

1. Header and navigation
2. Hero
3. Introduction
4. Services
5. Why choose us
6. How our support works
7. Service areas
8. Frequently asked questions
9. Contact call to action
10. Footer

The section order may vary when it improves
the design.

## Layout Variants

Allow reusable layouts including:

Hero:
- Split
- Full width
- Centred

Services:
- Three-column cards
- Two-column cards
- Image-led grid

Content:
- Image left
- Image right
- Full-width editorial

CTA:
- Gold background
- Charcoal background
- Minimal white section

## Accessibility

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA.

Check colour contrast for all text and controls.
Gold backgrounds should use readable dark text.
Provide visible focus states.
Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
