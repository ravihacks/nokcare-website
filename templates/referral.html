Who can make an enquiry?

Participants, family members, carers, support coordinators and other professionals can contact the team to discuss support.

Can I upload an NDIS plan or health documents?

This website does not accept uploads or participant records. Call the team first to arrange a suitable secure way to share documents.

What happens when I review the form?

The preview checks that the required fields are complete. It does not send or save your details, and it does not submit a referral.

How can I proceed now?

Call the team to confirm service availability and discuss a secure next step. You do not need to share personal documents to ask about services.
