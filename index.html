Ask us a question ↗
Where does NOK Care provide support?

Our listed service areas are Melbourne, Lara, Bannockburn, Geelong and Werribee. Contact us to discuss availability in your suburb.

What kind of support can I ask about?

Explore daily living, personal activities, household help, transport, community participation and NDIS services on our services page. Our team can discuss your individual needs and current availability.

Can my family or support coordinator get in touch?

You, a family member or a support coordinator can contact NOK Care to discuss the next steps. Please call before sharing any personal referral documents.

How do I make an NDIS referral?

Visit our referral page and call the team to discuss a secure way to proceed. Online referral collection is not yet available on this website.
